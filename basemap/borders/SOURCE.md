# Historical borders

Simplified from [aourednik/historical-basemaps](https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps), licensed under the [GNU GPL v3.0](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html). Changes: geometry simplified to about 1 km, small islands dropped, properties reduced to a name and a colour, one label point per polity added. Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build_borders.py`.
