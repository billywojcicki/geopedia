# Historical borders

Before 1886 (`<year>.geojson`, `bc<year>.geojson`): simplified from [aourednik/historical-basemaps](https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps), licensed under the [GNU GPL v3.0](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html). Changes: geometry simplified to about 1 km, small islands dropped, properties reduced to a name and a colour, one label point per polity added. Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build_borders.py`.

From 1886 (`cshapes.json`): adapted from [CShapes 2.0](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/cshapes/) (Schvitz, Guy, Luc Girardin, Seraina Rüegger, Nils B. Weidmann, Lars-Erik Cederman and Kristian Skrede Gleditsch. 2022. "Mapping the International System, 1886-2019: The CShapes 2.0 Dataset." Journal of Conflict Resolution 66(1)), licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/); this file is shared under the same licence. Changes: borders simplified to about 1 km, small islands dropped, outlines that never stood on a 1 July dropped, names shortened to common English names (some set by date), colonies coloured by their ruler, one label point per outline added, re-encoded as a compact topology. Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build_cshapes.py`.
